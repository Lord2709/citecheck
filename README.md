# CiteCheck

A paper-judging tool for researchers. Submit a paper (link) along with a short note on your research direction, and get back a verdict on two things:

1. **Relevance** - does this paper actually fit your research direction?
2. **Citation support** - do the papers it cites genuinely support the claims they are attached to?

Built for students and researchers who read and cite papers weekly, and want a faster way to triage what's worth a full read versus what's not, and to catch citations that don't hold up.
