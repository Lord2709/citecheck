# Consent script (read aloud; note the date and their yes/no in the roster)

"Thanks for helping. We're students testing a tool that checks whether a paper supports a claim. It's the **tool** that's being tested, not you.

- It takes about 20 minutes. You can stop at any time, no reason needed.
- I'll take notes and a screenshot, and the app keeps a log of what you click and how long tasks take.
- I'll refer to you only as a code like 'P03'. Your name and e-mail won't be in our repository or report.
- Would you be OK with the app also saving the **text you type** (for example your claims), with e-mail addresses and phone numbers removed? You can say no and everything else still works.
- Would you be OK with me recording your **screen** (no camera, no audio)? You can say no.

Do you agree to take part? (yes / no)   Text logging? (yes / no)   Screen recording? (yes / no)"

Record the answers in `roster.csv` (`consent_participation`, `consent_text_logging`, `consent_screen_recording`, `consent_date`).
If they say no to participation, thank them and stop: collect nothing.
